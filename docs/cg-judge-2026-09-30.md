# CrazyGames judge review — 2026-09-30

Build reviewed: `bash tools/package/build-crazygames.sh` from the current working tree (version string `4.4.1`, 276 files, 9.5MB zipped). This is newer than build 8 that was rejected on 29.09 (it includes the unshipped Public Server), so a few findings may not apply to the exact submitted build; none of the major ones depend on that difference.

Screenshots are in `tools/review/shots/`. Every file named below lives there.

## Verdict

The reviewer almost certainly rejected the game because, in the first three minutes, it reads as a **silent, dark, sprawling hobby project with no single thing to do**. On CrazyGames there is no music at all, and every sound effect is a synthesized beep, so the first impression is near-silence. The home screen offers three big choices, four more cards hidden below the fold, and seven unlabeled icon buttons. The most prominent button ("Start Chapter 1") plays about 18 seconds of narrated text, says "Fight back", then drops you in an empty backyard with "FIND: Exit · 509m". The tutorial is a punching-bag drill that ends back on the menu without ever giving you an opponent. Quick Fight is fast, but what it shows is two small, dark, thin-outlined stickmen in a soup of rings, floating numbers and blurred banners under a HUD band that takes a fifth of the screen. Everything the game is proud of (the bosses, the adaptive AI, domains, cinematics) is locked behind a 186-chapter story, so a three-minute reviewer never sees it. Meanwhile CrazyGames' new stickman and action releases get the player into one obvious loop within seconds, and the stickman-fighter slot is already held by established games (Stickman Clash has 156K likes). The quality bar the reviewer is applying is not "is this polished?". Blocky Sword and Banner Brawl are visually cruder than us. The bar is "does this feel like a focused, finished product in the first minute?", and we fail that.

## How this was tested

- The bundle was served locally. Chrome was launched headless with a fresh, empty profile and `--host-resolver-rules=MAP smoke.game-files.crazygames.com 127.0.0.1`, so the game ran its on-portal code paths. The console confirmed `[MusicManager] External music disabled in this environment.`
- The game ran inside an iframe on a mock CrazyGames page, in a 1366x711 viewport (a 1366x768 laptop minus browser chrome). I later measured the real CrazyGames game frame at that viewport: it is **918x470**, smaller than the 1040x585 iframe used for most of my shots. The `50-realframe-*` shots use the real size. Everything looks smaller and more cut off on the portal than in the other shots.
- Deviation from the brief: the agent-chrome MCP server is pinned to a persistent profile and cannot take `--host-resolver-rules`, so I drove a separate Chrome with agent-chrome's bundled puppeteer-core over the DevTools port instead.
- Fights were handed to `SovereignControl.toggle()`. The tutorial was played with the keyboard. For the attack step I teleported the fighter next to the dummy, because platforming through a remote driver was unreliable. The Creator boss was launched with `selectMode('boss'); startGame()`, because no player can reach it.
- Limits: headless Chrome has no audio output, so I could not listen to anything. Audio findings come from the bundle contents, the code and the console. I did not finish story chapter 1, the boss or Battle Royale.

## Timeline of a first-time player

These were measured from a clean load in the mock frame.

| Moment | Time / clicks |
|---|---|
| Splash ("click to continue", which advances on its own) | 0–2.5s |
| Home menu plus "First time here?" tutorial modal | about 3.5s |
| Quick Fight path: "Not now", then Quick Fight, then the 2-second VERSUS splash | **2 clicks, about 7–8s to combat** |
| Tutorial path: "Play Tutorial" | 1 click, about 5s to controlling a fighter, but there is never an opponent. The tutorial ends back on the menu. |
| Story path: "Start Chapter 1" | 1 click, then about 18s of narration, then about 10s of walking before the first enemy appears (**about 30s**). The narration ends "Fight back", but there is nobody to fight. |

Loading is not the problem. The problem is which path a newcomer is pushed toward and what they find on it.

## Problems, ranked by likely contribution to the rejection

Each problem is marked **Verified** (I saw it) or **Suspected** (inferred from code or a partial observation).

### 1. No music, and every sound effect is a synthesized beep (Verified)

- **Observed:** The CrazyGames bundle contains **zero audio files**. `find dist/crazygames -name '*.mp3' -o -name '*.ogg' -o -name '*.wav'` returns nothing. `MusicManager` is a no-op stub on CrazyGames (`js/smb-audio.js:176`); the console logged it. All sound effects are oscillator and noise bursts (`js/smb-audio.js:96-105`). For example, `hit()` is a 120Hz square wave plus 0.1s of white noise.
- **Inferred:** On the portal the game is effectively silent apart from chiptune clicks. Every competitor I opened ships real music and sampled effects. A silent fighting game reads as unfinished within 10 seconds. I could not hear the effects, so "cheap" is inferred from how they are made, not from listening.
- **Fix:** License or commission 2–3 royalty-free music loops (menu, fight, boss) and about 15 sampled effects (swing, light, heavy and blunt hit, block, KO, jump, land, UI click, super). Ship them as files and replace the CrazyGames music stub with a local-file player. This also removes the YouTube music dependency on every other host.

### 2. No hook: the front door sends newcomers into the weakest content (Verified)

- **Observed:**
  - The home screen offers "Start Chapter 1" (the orange call to action), "Quick Fight" and "The Simulator". Four more mode cards (Story, Battle Royale, Versus, Online) sit **entirely below the fold in the real 918x470 frame**, and nothing shows that the page scrolls (`50-realframe-menu.png`). There are also seven unlabeled icon buttons (`03-fresh-10.png`). "Story" and "Start Chapter 1" overlap, as do "Versus", "Quick Fight" and "The Simulator". The Simulator opens yet another layer, "Refight / The Grid" (`27-simulator.png`).
  - Story chapter 1 promises a fight but delivers a walk. The narration ends "They stepped through a tear in reality and they're looking right at you. Fight back." (`13-story-11.png`). Clicking Fight! drops you in a suburban backyard with "FIND: Exit · 509m" and no enemy (`15-story-fight-05.png`). The first enemy shows up about 10 seconds of walking later (`16-story-walk-12.png`).
  - The tutorial never pits you against an opponent and ends on the menu with a toast covering the logo (`08-tut-end.png`).
  - Every boss is locked: "Beat each encounter in Story Mode to unlock it here" (`28-refight.png`). The menu footer teases "A secret power lies dormant… beat the boss to uncover it" (`21-home-scrolled.png`). The progress line reads "0 / 186 CHAPTERS".
- **Inferred:** A three-minute reviewer sees a menu maze, a text cutscene and an empty yard. The best material, the Creator fight (`29-boss-01.png`, `29-boss-10.png`), with speech bubbles, a dark arena and real menace, is unreachable. "186 chapters" signals sprawl, not value.
- **Fix:** On first launch, skip the menu. Run a single 60–90 second scripted first fight against a real bot, with the controls taught inline as prompts. End it on a boss-flavored beat, such as a Creator cameo with a short burst of the boss arena. Only then show the menu. Let anyone start one boss fight from the menu, for example as a "Boss Rush" teaser for the Creator.

### 3. Fights are hard to read at real size (Verified)

- **Observed:**
  - Fighters are black-filled figures with a thin neon outline, about 70–90px tall in the real frame. On dark arenas (night city, sewer, boss arena, the tutorial's navy void) they nearly disappear (`50-realframe-fight2.png`, `04-tut-02.png`).
  - The HUD is a full-width band that takes about 18% of the play area on desktop and about 27% on phone landscape (`30-phone-land-fight.png`).
  - That band is a frosted-glass blur of the scene behind it. Ability callouts like "GHOST STEP" render inside it as **blurred, unreadable text** (`17-story-sov-08.png`, `10-qf-fight-01.png`, `08-tut-super-a.png`). A hard 1px line at the band's bottom edge cuts across every arena.
  - Combat moments become a soup of overlapping rings, damage numbers ("-27", "-33" and "+25" stacked), particles and translucent shields (`10-qf-fight-01.png`, `10-qf-fight-06.png`, `29-boss-10.png`). The "COMEBACK!" banner is clipped by the HUD band (`10-qf-fight-09.png`).
  - Most arenas have an unexplained red vertical line and a tinted band near the left edge (`09-qf-03.png`, `30-phone-land-fight.png`, `08-tut-super-a.png`).
- **Fix:**
  - Give fighters a bright fill or rim light and a 3–4px outline, and add about 15% camera zoom in 1v1.
  - Collapse the HUD into compact corner bars and drop the full-width band and its blur.
  - Draw callouts above the HUD, not inside the blur.
  - Merge damage numbers per target within a short window, and limit concurrent rings.
  - Find and remove the red edge line, or explain it if it is a hazard.

### 4. It looks like several games stitched together (Verified)

- **Observed:**
  - There are three character art styles. Gameplay uses neon-outlined stick figures. Story scenes use a blue bean-shaped cartoon with googly eyes (`13-story-00.png`). The tutorial dummy is a gray block figure with its name printed twice ("DUMMY" above the health bar and "DUMMY" again under it, `05-tut-jump.png`).
  - Fonts mix Arial (the tutorial modal and the "Fracture Scout" and "OBLITERATE ×35" banners), a monospace face (tutorial prompts), a Bungee-style display face (Story Mode header) and the Rajdhani UI face. Compare `03-fresh-06.png`, `05-tut-jump.png`, `20-story-menu-d.png` and `29-boss-10.png`.
  - Joke weapons (Broomstick, Frying Pan, Paper Airplane, Pea Shooter) sit beside a grim "premium" presentation. The tutorial randomly handed me "Broomstick · Archer" (`04-tut-02.png`).
  - Developer residue shows: "v4.4.1" in the menu corner, "Settings v4.4.1" and "Updates v4.4.1" on setup screens (`22-br-3.png`), and a "Welcome back, Player 1" greeting on a first visit (`27-simulator.png`).
  - Text overlaps in many places:
    - The Terms footer sits over the mode cards (`03-fresh-06.png`).
    - The purple crystal covers "0 / 186 CHAPTERS" (`50-realframe-menu.png`).
    - On the phone landscape menu, the icon buttons cover the title (`30-phone-land-menu2.png`).
    - Battle Royale's minimap labels overlap into an unreadable smear ("Outskirts City Cr…").
    - Battle Royale's edge markers stack ("P40 P53 P35", `23-br-play-10.png`).
- **Fix:** Unify the story-scene figure with the gameplay rig. Cap the game at two fonts. Remove the joke weapons from the random and default pools on the portal build, or reskin them. Strip version strings from player-facing UI. Fix the overlaps listed above.

### 5. The tutorial looks broken in places (Verified)

- **Observed:**
  - The training-mode debug toolbar (HP, SUPER, CLEAR, MAP, gear) shows during the tutorial (`05-tut-jump.png`).
  - For the attack step, the dummy stands on a raised platform **under the instruction panel**, so the target is hidden behind the text telling you to hit it (`06-tut-hit.png`). You need a double jump to reach it, the tutorial hasn't fully taught that yet, and hits knock the dummy off the platform, so you chase it (`07-tut-after-attack.png`).
  - About 1 in 3 swings landed for me.
  - The final step says "Weapons, classes and domains build on these six" and then returns you to the menu (`08-tut-end.png`).
- **Fix:** Hide the training toolbar while the tutorial gate is active. Place the dummy on the floor in clear view and pin it against knockback. Move the prompt panel to the top edge. End the tutorial by starting a real bot fight. Most of this is subsumed by Fix 2.

### 6. Story onboarding is out of order and the copy contradicts itself (Verified)

- **Observed:**
  - Pressing Menu mid-chapter shows **"DRAW!"** with a "Spirit lingers where you fell" card: "Nothing struck that last blow. The world took you: a fall, or the ground itself" (`18-after-story-menu.png`). The player had simply quit.
  - Opening Story Menu after already playing chapter 1 plays a *second* prologue ("Every universe has a seam", plain Arial on black, `20-story-menu.png`). Only after that does it ask for a difficulty (`20-story-menu-c.png`). That difficulty applies to a chapter you've already played.
  - The chapter screen says "ten acts to the truth" and "Act 1 of 1" at the same time (`20-story-menu-d.png`).
  - In chapter 1 a tall orange lightning column sat in place while the AI-controlled fighter lost a life near it (`17-story-sov-04.png`). Whether it is a hazard, a gate or the goal beacon was not clear from the screen. That part is Suspected.
- **Fix:** Ask for difficulty (or default to Normal) before the first chapter starts. Play one prologue only. On voluntary quit, show "Chapter paused" with Resume / Restart / Leave, not a death card. Fix the act count string.

### 7. Online is empty to a reviewer (Verified locally; the live state is Suspected)

- **Observed:** The Online screen shows "No open rooms right now." The Public Server button spends about 20 seconds on "Connecting to the Public Server…" and then reports "not available" (`26-pubserver-0.png` through `26-pubserver-7.png`). The online setup screen also shows a "PLAYER 2 · BOT" loadout panel that means nothing online, and its START GAME button sits under the Terms footer (`25-online-1.png`).
- **Inferred:** The memory notes say the hub is not deployed on Render, and the submitted build didn't include it. Either way, a reviewer clicking Online sees nothing to do. This hurts more than having no Online card at all.
- **Fix:** On the portal, hide Online until the hub is live and populated. Alternatively, make the hub always populated, with bots filling empty slots, so clicking it always puts you in a fight within seconds.

### 8. One click on Menu abandons the match with no confirmation (Verified)

- **Observed:** "⌂ Menu" sits top-center during every fight. One click ends Quick Fight (`12-ingame-menu-click.png`) and story chapters (`18-after-story-menu.png`). It is easy to hit while aiming at the HUD.
- **Fix:** Make it a pause button that opens Resume / Settings / Quit, with a confirmation on Quit.

### 9. Battle Royale is the most marketable mode but looks unfinished (Verified)

- **Observed:** Its setup screen is the generic Versus layout, including a Player 2 "BOT" panel with a sad red blob preview and hat/cape dropdowns set to "None" (`22-br-3.png`). In the match, two biome backgrounds meet in a hard vertical seam (`23-br-play-10.png`), the inventory hotbar sits over the fighters' feet, and the minimap and edge-marker text overlap.
- **Fix:** Give it a dedicated one-screen setup: pick a weapon, then DROP. Fix the label collisions, and add a cross-fade or a prop at biome seams.

### 10. Phone layout (Verified)

- **Observed:** In phone landscape, the HUD takes about 27% of the height, the touch buttons cover the lower corners of the arena, the PAUSE pill sits over the bot, and "⌂ Menu" and "PAUSE" both appear (`30-phone-land-fight.png`). The home menu is cut off at the bottom, with "Start Chapter 1" half off-screen (`30-phone-land-menu2.png`). Portrait mode scrolls, but the Terms footer overlaps the cards (`30-phone-port-menu.png`).
- **Fix:** Shrink the HUD and make the touch controls semi-transparent. Pick one pause entry point. Make the landscape menu fit with no scroll.

### 11. Minor or unconfirmed

- **Suspected (likely a harness artifact):** two real mouse clicks on the story scene's canvas "Fight!" button did not advance it, but a synthetic click event did (`14-story-fight-04.png`). Worth one manual check on a real laptop.
- **Verified:** the console prints save and story debug logs (`[STORY FLUSH]`, `[SAVE WRITE]`, `[SAVE VERIFIED]` and similar) every 15 seconds during play. No game exceptions appeared in the windows I captured. The only errors were the mock page's favicon 404 and "server unreachable" from the local origin.

## Strengths to protect while fixing

- Load time: about 3.5s to an interactive menu, with a 9.5MB bundle. Don't regress this with the audio pass. Stream the music after the menu appears.
- Quick Fight: 2 clicks, about 2s of VERSUS splash, then combat (`09-qf-00.png`). The end screen, with a match summary and an auto "Play Again (7)" countdown, is clean (`10-qf-fight-18.png`).
- The Creator boss presentation (`29-boss-01.png`) and the colosseum arena art (`09-qf-03.png`) are the best-looking things in the build. Push them forward instead of cutting them.
- Touch controls exist and work in phone landscape.
- No runtime exceptions in about 25 minutes of play across six modes.

## The 5 changes most likely to get it accepted

1. **Real audio on the portal.** Add 2–3 music loops plus about 15 sampled sound effects as shipped files, and replace the CrazyGames music stub. Effort: about 2–3 days including sourcing; the code change is about half a day.
2. **A first-run flow that is a fight, not a menu.** First launch goes straight into a scripted 60–90 second bot fight with inline control prompts, ending on a short Creator cameo. Then show the menu, and make one boss fight playable from it. This also replaces the current tutorial and fixes problems 2 and 5. Effort: about 4–6 days.
3. **A readability pass on combat.** Brighter, thicker fighters; a 1v1 camera zoom; corner HUD bars instead of the blurred band; callouts drawn on top; merged damage numbers and fewer rings; remove the red edge line. Effort: about 3–5 days.
4. **A focused menu that fits 918x470.** One hero PLAY button and at most three modes visible without scrolling (Fight, Story, Battle Royale). Labeled icons. No version strings. Online hidden until it's populated. Fix the footer overlap. Effort: about 2–3 days.
5. **A consistency and cleanup sweep.** Use the gameplay figure in story scenes, cap fonts at two, take joke weapons out of the default pool, add pause-with-confirm instead of instant Menu, replace "DRAW!" on quit, ask for difficulty before chapter 1 with a single prologue, fix "Act 1 of 1", and give Battle Royale its own setup screen. Effort: about 3–4 days.

Changes 1 and 2 carry most of the weight. Without them, I don't expect the other three to change the verdict.

## Directly observed versus inferred

- **Directly observed in this session:** load timings, click counts, every screen described above, the silent-music log line, the empty audio bundle, the blurred callouts, the overlaps, the tutorial toolbar and dummy placement, the DRAW-on-quit card, the double prologue and late difficulty prompt, "Act 1 of 1", the empty Online screen with its failing Public Server, the instant Menu quit, the real frame size of 918x470, and the competitor screens.
- **Inferred:** that the sound effects sound cheap (from the code, not from listening); why the reviewer rejected the game (from the gap between our first three minutes and the competitors'); the effort estimates; the orange column's role in chapter 1; and whether the canvas Fight! button fails for real users.
