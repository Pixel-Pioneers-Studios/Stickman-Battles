# "Can my AI beat my game?" — what works

## Ep 1 vs Ep 2 (read Nov 8 2026, yt-check)

| | Ep 1 "An AI Is Playing My Character." | Ep 2 "My AI Keeps Dying Here." |
|---|---|---|
| Stayed to watch | **45.8%** (992 views) | **17.1%** (301 views) |
| Length | 20.2 s (14.1 s footage + 6.1 s end card) | 15.2 s (9.1 s footage + 6.1 s end card) |
| First line | "Someone's playing me. And it's not you." | "Chapter seven. It keeps dying here." |
| Footage | ONE run, start to finish | THREE different runs spliced |
| HP counter | 163 → 149 → 63 → 45 → 31, only goes down | 42 → 1 → 14 → 1 → 61 → 40, jumps around |
| Frame 0 | bright rooftop, sword spin, HP full | dark orange street, HP already 42 (red) |

The same layout, narrator, voice, music and end card were in both, so those are not the difference.

What changed:

1. **The hook was written for people who saw Ep 1.** "Chapter seven" and "it" mean nothing to a stranger, and the word "AI" is never on screen or spoken in the opening; it is only in the title, which the Shorts feed barely shows. Ep 1's first line is a premise any stranger understands in one second: the game character says someone is playing him and it isn't you. Almost every Shorts viewer is new, so every episode needs its own premise in the first second.
2. **No single stake to follow.** Ep 1 is one run: full HP, it bleeds down, "ONE LEFT.", it wins. Ep 2 cuts between three runs, so the HP counter (the thing the eye tracks) resets twice and the win at the end is from a different run. That reads like a cheat, and it gives the viewer no reason to wait.
3. **It opens already lost.** HP 42 in red on frame 0, dark low-contrast arena, small dark fighters. Ep 1 opens on a bright, readable hit.
4. **The end card is 40% of Ep 2** (6.1 of 15.2 s) vs 30% of Ep 1. Secondary: stayed% is decided in the first seconds, but a long black card hurts loops.
5. Ep 2 went out the same evening as Ep 1, so its feed test was smaller (301 views). This changes how many people saw it, not the 17% rate.

## Rules for every episode

- First line + first caption = a stand-alone premise with "AI" in it, not an episode reference ("Chapter N" goes later).
- One continuous run. The HP counter only ever shows one run. If the run is lost, the episode is about the loss.
- Frame 0: full HP, an active hit, readable fighters.
- End card <= ~4 s.
- Space episodes >= 1 day apart.
